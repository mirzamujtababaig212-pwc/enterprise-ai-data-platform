# Enterprise AI Platform
## Vision
The Enterprise AI Platform is a cloud-native, modular platform designed to support enterprise data engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and business applications through a 
secure, scalable, and observable architecture.
The platform provides a unified foundation for building and operating enterprise-grade data pipelines, AI services, knowledge systems, and intelligent agents across multiple business domains.
---
# Objectives
- Build reusable enterprise services.
- Support batch and real-time data processing.
- Enable AI-powered business applications.
- Provide secure enterprise integrations.
- Deliver production-grade observability.
- Simplify deployment across multiple cloud providers.
